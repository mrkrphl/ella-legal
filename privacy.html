Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Ella is a client for an agent server that runs on your own Mac. We do not run a server that holds your conversations, and we do not have accounts.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracking of any kind in the app. There is no sign-up and there are no credentials of ours to lose.

What stays on your devices

  • The pairing token. When you scan the code on your Mac, Ella receives a device token and keeps it in the iOS Keychain. It is sent only to the Mac you paired with.
  • Which Mac you paired with. The address is kept in the app's own preferences on your phone so it can reconnect.
  • Your conversations. Chats live on your Mac, in the agent server's database. Ella holds them in memory while a thread is open and nothing else.

What leaves your devices, and to whom

Two things, both because you set them up:

  • Your phone to your Mac. Chats travel over your own private Tailscale network to the Mac you paired with. Nobody else's server is in the path.
  • Your Mac to the AI provider you chose. The Mac side answers a message by calling a model endpoint that you configured there — for example DeepSeek or xAI. What you type, and the parts of the screen an agent is allowed to see, are sent to that provider under that provider's terms and privacy policy. Ella itself never calls a model provider, and we never see any of it.

If you would rather nothing leave your Mac, the Mac side can be pointed at a model that runs locally, and the agents still work.

Camera and local network

  • Camera is used for one thing: scanning the pairing code shown on your Mac. No photo is stored or uploaded.
  • Local network is used to reach your Mac when it is on the same Wi-Fi. Over Tailscale the traffic is end-to-end between your own devices.

Screen and actions

An agent may be allowed to look at your Mac's screen, and — only with a switch turned on and a yes you give — to click and type. Both are decisions you make on the Mac. They are not recorded by us, and nothing is sent anywhere except the result of the action, inside the conversation on your Mac.

Children

Ella is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting what is kept

Sign a device out on the Mac to revoke its token, or delete the app to remove it from the phone. Deleting a chat on the Mac deletes it; there is no second copy for us to keep.
