Ella

Your agents, in your pocket.

Ella is the iPhone app for agents that run on your own Mac. No account, no cloud of ours, nothing to subscribe to — your Mac does the work and Ella is the window into it.

The Mac side runs here, at home, and reaches your phone privately over Tailscale. Ella talks only to that Mac. Each agent has its own chat, its own memory and its own switches; anything that would touch your screen waits for your yes.

What it is

  • · Chat with the agents on your Mac, from anywhere.
  • · Group several agents into one room and let them answer.
  • · Approve or refuse a screen action from the phone.
  • · Stop an answer while it is still arriving.

What it is not

  • Not a service. There is no Ella server of ours holding your conversations.
  • Not a subscription. No in-app purchases at all.
  • Not a screen recorder. An agent sees the screen only when you ask it to.
