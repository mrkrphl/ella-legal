Support

Ella needs a Mac to talk to. This page is what to check when the two cannot find each other.

What you need

  • An iPhone on iOS 17 or later.
  • A Mac with the agent server installed, running, and awake.
  • Tailscale on both, signed in to the same network. This is what lets your phone reach your Mac from anywhere without opening a port to the internet.

How pairing works

  • On the Mac: start the agent server, then open the pairing panel.
  • On the phone: Pair with your Mac, then scan the code — or paste the link if the camera cannot be used.
  • The code is one-time and expires in 15 minutes. A new one is a new device.

When it will not connect

  • "Connecting…" forever. Is the Mac awake, and is the server started?
  • Tailscale off on either side. Open Tailscale on both and check both are signed in to the same tailnet.
  • Mac asleep. A laptop that is asleep cannot answer. Wake it, or keep it awake while you are away.
  • Rejected token. The Mac was reinstalled, or the device was signed out. Pair again with a new code.

Cut access

  • Sign the phone out on the Mac to revoke its token immediately.
  • Delete the app to remove it from the phone.
  • Revoke the Mac's Tailscale device if you no longer trust it.
